Privacy Policy – Tracking Community (Chrome Extension) Last updated: 28 Jan 2026 What data we handle The extension stores the following only on your device (Chrome local storage): your watchlist (movies and TV shows), viewing progress, notes, “continue watching” and rewatch data. We do not send this data to our servers. Third‑party services • TMDB (themoviedb.org): we send only search queries and content IDs (e.g. show or movie IDs) to fetch titles, posters and metadata. This is necessary for search and display. We do not send any personal data. See TMDB’s privacy policy for their practices. We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone for advertising or marketing. Permissions • Storage: to save your watchlist, progress and preferences locally. • Scripting / host access: to show the extension panel on web pages. We do not read or collect page content. We do not use remote code. Data export and deletion You can export your data from the extension menu (⋮ → Esporta). All data stays in your browser.
